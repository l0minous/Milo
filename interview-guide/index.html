What’s unique about our early career program?

We are so excited to welcome another cohort of early career folks to Notion! We’ve crafted an early career specific interview process to showcase your strengths, as well as give you a better sense of the technical challenges we solve here at Notion.

We know that mentorship is incredibly important at this stage in your career. Upon joining, we’ll make sure you’re paired with a senior mentor/buddy that can work with you to develop your skills and amplify your impact.

Overall Interview Process & What to Expect

💻

Note that all interviews will be conducted virtually over Google Meet.

  1. 👋

    Initial Interview

    15 min

    An interview with a member of our Talent team to get to know you. We want to learn about the highlights of your journey so far and what you're looking for, and to tell you about what we're working on at Notion now and in the future.

  2. Take Home Assessment

    3 hours

    For this interview exercise, you'll build a small version of Notion's @-mention menu—the UI that helps you quickly insert references to pages, people, or dates as you type. We'll give you a starter React and TypeScript repo that renders a basic input and an intentionally plain results list, plus a simple pages search endpoint (in the repo) and a hosted people search endpoint you can query.

    Your goal is to take this skeleton and extend it in a focused, high-quality way. You can choose what to implement—examples include better rendering/token styling, keyboard navigation, accessibility, performance under fast typing (debouncing/cancellation/out-of-order responses), adding people results, adding date handling (e.g. @today, @tomorrow), and/or ranking across mixed result types.

    We care most about product taste, reasoning, and engineering fundamentals: making deliberate trade-offs, building something correct and polished, and explaining what you chose to leave out. Along with the code, please include a short write-up (≤500 words) describing what you built, key decisions, and what you'd do next with more time.

  3. Virtual Onsite

    2 hours

    While we are big fans of AI and the potential it unlocks, we kindly ask you refrain from using tools like ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, or agentic coding tools or similar to generate code during the interview as we care deeply about ensuring a consistent and fair experience for all. AI Overview answers from Google are fine to use when looking up questions or documentation.

    Coding Interview 60 min

    • In this interview, we're going to ask you to write code to solve a problem. No knowledge of specific libraries or technologies will be tested here. You will receive a CodeSignal link in your email confirmation. No further preparation is needed.
    • We encourage asking any clarification questions and make sure you understand the problem at the onset. Break down the problem and talk through your approach before starting to code.
    • You're welcome to look up anything you need on Google, StackOverflow, etc, and any common libraries can all be used. Make sure you're communicating what you're doing or thinking to the interviewer, especially if you get stuck. Part of this interview is we want to see what it would be like to work with you.
    • You're not required to write tests, but writing them can help assert the correctness of your code. Think about edge cases and how to adapt your solution to cover them.

    Debugging Interview 60 min

    • You will spend the first 15 minutes going over your take home. Expect to give a high level overview on your code, why you chose your specific path, product thinking, trade offs, etc.
    • For the remaining 45 minutes, you will work on debugging an existing application written in TypeScript and a bit of the React frontend framework. We'll primarily be looking at how you go about investigating and fixing bugs in unfamiliar code. In addition, we want to see how you collaborate with others. (If at any point you notice ways to improve our code, test coverage, or readability, please share them with your interviewer!) The goal is to simulate real-world engineering.
    • Although the code is written in React/TypeScript, which is what Notion uses, you don't have to be an expert in React/TypeScript to be successful in this interview. If you need any help with syntax, the interviewer would be happy to help. During the interview, you are also welcome to look up anything you need on Google, StackOverflow, etc., but we do not allow the use of AI development tooling (e.g., Claude Code).
    • You will be working locally for this interview; you will need Node.js v22. If you're struggling to get the setup working on Windows, WSL is helpful (you can run it on Linux instead, which works better). Nvm is a helpful utility if you have multiple node versions on your machine.
    📦 env-check.zip Download to confirm your environment is ready

  4. Career History

    45 min

    This final conversation will be with an engineering manager of one of our engineering teams. (This does not mean you will be hired on to this team, as this will come after all early career folks have been hired and we have a generalized matching process.)

    You'll be meeting with an engineering leader at Notion to discuss your career history. You may find it helpful to review how your past teams operated and how you collaborated with others. Reflect on what went well, what could have been improved, and successes and challenges you faced throughout your career. Consider how you interacted with fellow engineers, product managers, engineering managers, designers, etc.

  5. 🌱

    Values Interview

    30 min

    An interview where you'll discuss your past experience, values and motivations with a member of our leadership team. This is also a great opportunity to learn more about how we're thinking about growth for the product and company. If you haven't already, we encourage you to read through Notion’s operating values.

  6. ✒️

    Reference & Background Checks

    Lastly, as a final step in the process, we'll also conduct reference checks. All offers are contingent on a background check, which includes employment verification.

We want everyone who interviews with us to feel set up for success. Please reach out if you have questions!
